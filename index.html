    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is my PDF uploaded to any server?

    No. Your PDF is read and rendered locally inside your browser using Mozilla's PDF.js engine. Your files, text, and exported images never leave your device. See the Privacy Policy for complete details.

    Should I choose PNG, JPG, or WebP?

    Choose PNG for documents with small text, tables, diagrams, or transparency — it is completely lossless. Choose JPG for photo-heavy pages where smaller file sizes and universal compatibility matter. Choose WebP for modern web usage where you want smaller file sizes than PNG/JPG and transparency support.

    What DPI should I select?

    150 DPI is great for quick screen sharing and slides. 300 DPI (recommended) is the standard for crisp print and sharp text reading. 600 DPI or custom values up to 1200 DPI are ideal for fine archival detail and high-magnification artwork.

    How are the output pixel dimensions calculated?

    Pixel width = (page width in PDF points ÷ 72) × DPI, and the same formula applies to height. A standard US Letter page (612 × 792 pt) renders at exactly 2,550 × 3,300 px at 300 DPI.

    Can I convert a password-protected PDF?

    Yes. When you select an encrypted PDF, a modal will ask for the password. It is used strictly in memory to unlock the file locally and is never stored or transmitted.

    Privacy Policy

    How Owl PDF to Image Converter handles your documents: everything stays on your device.

    100% Local Processing

    This application runs entirely inside your web browser. When you select a PDF, the file is read directly into browser memory and rendered onto an HTML5 canvas. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server.

    Zero Data Collection

    • The contents of your PDFs (text, vector graphics, images, and metadata) are never transmitted anywhere.
    • Previews and converted images exist only as temporary Blob URLs in your active tab's memory.
    • Passwords entered for encrypted PDFs are used only once in memory to unlock the document and are immediately cleared.

    Memory Cleanup

    Closing the tab, clicking "Remove", or choosing "Convert Another PDF" immediately destroys the PDF instance and revokes all temporary download URLs from browser memory.

    About Owl PDF to Image Converter

    A precision client-side tool for turning PDF pages into high-resolution PNG, JPG, and WebP images.

    How Rendering Works

    Documents are parsed and rendered using PDF.js. For every selected page, the target resolution is calculated as (page points ÷ 72) × DPI and drawn directly from the PDF's vector instructions onto a hardware-accelerated canvas. Standard font maps and character maps (CMaps) are loaded so typography renders accurately.

    Pages are processed sequentially one by one to keep RAM usage low and stable even on mobile devices.
